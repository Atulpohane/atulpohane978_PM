Case study 01

Meesho — Rebuilding trust & repeat orders

How might Meesho improve customer experience in Tier 2 & Tier 3 cities after late deliveries and product mismatches contributed to a reported 25% drop in repeat orders?

25%reported drop in repeat orders
100+reviews analyzed
30+structured interviews / survey
Tier 2/3focus market
Repeatpurchase outcome

Discovery → insight

The fellowship project analyzed 100+ reviews across Play Store, App Store, Reddit and Facebook, plus around 30+ structured user interviews/survey.

  • Product mismatch created a gap between listing expectations and reality.
  • Users had trust concerns around sellers/resellers.
  • Returns/refunds and delayed deliveries created additional friction.

Prioritization

Product mismatch and platform trust were treated as Must items; engaging activity and return improvements as Should items, while some seller/delivery issues were Could items.

Solution direction

Proposed interventions included quality/trust labels, supplier demo clips and a Virtual Trial Room. The intent was to reduce uncertainty before purchase rather than only fix problems after delivery.

What I would measure

Repeat purchase rate as the core outcome, supported by return rate, conversion, CSAT/NPS, trust-label conversion and engagement with product videos.

Case study 02

CRED — Improving Rent Via Credit Card repeat usage

Diagnosing why a newly launched feature attracted interest but recurring transactions reportedly dropped by 35% within two months.

35%reported decline in recurring transactions

Problem diagnosis

The project mapped user friction across fees, processing time, rewards, reminders, privacy and reliability.

  • 2.5% rent transaction fee was identified as a major barrier.
  • Rewards/tokens were perceived as less meaningful than direct cashback.
  • Manual reminders and delayed notifications reduced convenience.
  • Security/privacy concerns and glitches could reduce trust.

Prioritization

Fee/transparency, rewards, reminders and PAN/privacy concerns were treated as Must-Have areas in the project’s MoSCoW framework.

Solution mapping

  • Automated rent receipts and notifications.
  • Fee reduction through bank partnerships or a subscription model.
  • Personalized rewards with instant redemption.
  • In-app ticket generation and tracking for failed transactions.

Success metrics

Retention was positioned as the primary outcome, supported by receipt/notification performance, reward redemption and satisfaction, and issue response/resolution metrics.

Case study 03 / Feature deep-dive

Meesho — Virtual Trial Room

A feature concept designed to reduce product mismatch by helping users visualize clothing and accessories before purchase.

#4priority among proposed solutions in the project

Opportunity

Product mismatch was prioritized as a Must-Have pain point. The proposed AI/AR experience lets users upload pictures, provide details and visualize products using a realistic model.

Experience

Upload picture → provide details → generate realistic model → visualize clothing/accessories.

Expected impact

  • Reduce guesswork.
  • Increase purchase confidence.
  • Reduce post-purchase dissatisfaction and returns.

Prioritization insight

The project scored solutions on Reach, Impact, Confidence and Effort. In-app Quality Badges ranked #1, Demo Clips #2, Try-On Demo #3 and Virtual Trial Room #4. This is a useful PM lesson: the most sophisticated feature is not automatically the highest-priority feature.
